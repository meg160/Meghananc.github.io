Statistics & Experimentation | Python

A/B Test Analysis:
Ad Campaign Effectiveness

Hypothesis test on a public dataset of 588,101 users. The ad lifted conversion from 1.79% to 2.55% (+43%, p < 0.001, 95% CI 0.59 to 0.94 points), with the gain concentrated among users who saw 51 or more ads. Includes breakdowns by ad exposure, day and hour, and a clear limitations section.

Tools: Python, pandas, statsmodels, matplotlib, Google Colab
